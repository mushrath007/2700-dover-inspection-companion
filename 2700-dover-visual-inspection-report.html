Important limitation

Arrows identify inspection targets, not confirmed defects. Listing photos are staged, brightened and selective. “No visible concern” means only that the image does not show an obvious defect; it is not a pass. Do not authorize or negotiate repairs from photos alone—use the licensed inspection, moisture readings, sewer video, permit records and written estimates.

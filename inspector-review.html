What caught our attention

Short concern list

Ceiling staining or possible leakage

Please confirm whether the ceiling marks and patched-looking areas are dry, what may have caused them, and whether any follow-up is needed. Photos 20–22, 25, 27–28.

Roof age and remaining life

Please note the approximate roof age/condition, remaining useful life, and the condition of the chimney, vents, flashing and skylights. Photos 1, 8, 12, 36–37.

Fresh paint and prior repairs

Several rooms or ceilings appear recently painted. Please note whether you saw signs of old leakage, patching, repaired cracks or concealed damage, and what repair history we should ask the seller about.

Porch and garage water concerns

Please confirm whether the porch roof boards, skylight/flashing and garage show active or previous leakage, rot or moisture damage. Photos 3, 29–33.

Lower level, grading and flood/water history

Please confirm whether you found basement/lower-level moisture, poor grading, drainage problems or signs of past water entry, and whether we should ask for flood or water-remediation history. Photos 27–28, 33–37.

Other report items

Please confirm the report includes the boiler/HVAC, electrical panel, basement flooring, possible asbestos material before disturbance, doors/windows, railings, ceiling fans, kitchen exhaust, water pressure, grading and tree concerns.

Tests and specialist checks

Please confirm these were addressed

  • Termite / wood-destroying insect inspection and result
  • Radon test and result
  • Sewer scope: sediment was mentioned, with no roots or cracks; please confirm function and any cleaning recommendation
  • Evidence of past flooding or recurring water entry
  • Any area you could not access or fully inspect
  • Anything that should be evaluated by a roofer, HVAC contractor, electrician, plumber, moisture specialist or environmental testing company

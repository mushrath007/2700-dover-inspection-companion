Purpose before pieces

Buy only what supports sleep, gathering, learning, prayer, storage or work. Leave open space on purpose.

Child-safe and washable

Nature, managed well

Use a few verified plants and one properly supported, lidded aquarium—not fragile bowls or many small tanks.

One aquarium is enough

Plan one lidded 10–20 gallon planted tank on a purpose-built level stand in the family room, away from direct sun. An adult should cycle and maintain it, use GFCI protection and a drip loop, and confirm the floor and stand can carry the filled weight. The image is a layout concept—not an electrical, structural or animal-care approval.

    Privacy boundary for the public repository

    Only publish generated room concepts after checking them for faces, readable documents, family photographs, addresses, logos, watermarks and embedded metadata. Keep private inspection uploads in the app’s local browser storage and exported backups—not in this repository.

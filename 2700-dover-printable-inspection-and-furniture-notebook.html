Seller furniture · strategy

Best items to ask about first

These items appear to fit the photographed rooms. Availability, ownership, brand, condition and inclusion are not confirmed.

  1. Dining table and six-chair set · photos 2, 15–16. Strong first ask because it already fits the dining area and seats a six-person family. Inspect joints, chair wobble and upholstery.
  2. Gray sectional / family-room seating · photos 9–11. More practical for children than the light-colored sofa. Buy only if odor-, pest-, smoke- and stain-free with supportive cushions.
  3. Hard bedroom furniture · photos 20–25. Dressers, nightstands, desks and twin bed frames can save moving cost because they already fit. Anchor all tall pieces. Decline mattresses.
  4. Desk and chair for the lower office · photos 5, 23 or 25. Ask about the best-sized desk; test chair height/support and leave room around baseboard heat and outlets.
  5. Porch dining set · photos 3, 29–30. Useful if metal joints are sound and there is no rust, mildew or cushion odor. Treat as a low-cost add-on.

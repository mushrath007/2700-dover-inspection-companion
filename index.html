Seller furniture decisions

Availability and ownership are unverified. Inspect, measure and document every agreed item.

Important data limits

Your browser can remove site storage if history/site data is cleared. Private/incognito mode may erase it sooner. Notes use local storage and photos use an IndexedDB device database. JSON export includes both and is the durable backup. Data does not automatically synchronize across phones because there is no account or public database.
